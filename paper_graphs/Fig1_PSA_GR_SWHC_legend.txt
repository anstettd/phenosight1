DRAFT LEGEND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Figure 1. Projected shoot area and growth rate of Mimulus maternal lines across a
soil water-holding-capacity (SWHC) gradient, by collection site and collection
year. (A) Projected shoot area (PSA) over the course of the experiment for each
of five watering regimes (20, 40, 60, 80 and 100% SWHC), shown separately for
populations collected in Oregon and southern California in 2010 and 2014. Lines
are means across maternal lines and shaded bands are standard errors across
maternal lines (n = 3 lines per site x year group, except Oregon 2014, n = 2).
Beneath each set of curves, -log10(P) for the contrast between each watering
regime and 100% SWHC at that time point, from a one-way ANOVA across all five
regimes followed by Tukey's HSD; the dotted horizontal line marks P = 0.05.
(B) Growth rate for the early and late intervals, with letters indicating Tukey
HSD groupings within each panel (regimes sharing a letter do not differ at
P < 0.05) and the one-way ANOVA P value given above. Small symbols are individual
maternal-line means; large symbols are the mean +/- SE across maternal lines.
(C) Growth rate regressed on % SWHC within each site x year group, for the early
and late intervals; R2 and P of each regression are given in the panel. In all
panels the vertical dashed line at 409.5 h marks the point at which the plants
outgrew the imaging trays and the setup was changed; the discontinuity there is
technical and not biological.


HOW EACH QUANTITY WAS CALCULATED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Unit of replication
    The maternal line (Population, A-K). Every value plotted is first reduced to
    a maternal-line mean, so the replicate throughout is the line, not the plant.
    This follows the line-mean convention of 0_data_setup.R.

Time cut-off
    All series start at 144 h (TOE_MIN = 140 in the scripts). Projected shoot
    area reaches its minimum at exactly 144 h in all four site x year groups;
    before that point the per-plant splines are erratic on very small plants.
    52 time points are retained, on a 12 h grid.

Projected shoot area (PSA)
    The Roi.Mask channel of the PhenoSight export, renamed PSA in 0_data_setup.R.
    Panel A plots the mean of the maternal-line means per regime at each time
    point, with SE taken across maternal lines.

Growth rate (GR)
    Fitted per plant as the slope of lm(PSA ~ TOE) over that plant's own
    interval, then averaged to a maternal-line mean. Plants were kept only if
    R2 > 0.7 and GR > 0, the same filter used for the original growth files.
    Fitting per plant and averaging afterwards (rather than fitting one slope to
    the line-mean series) preserves within-line variation and allows the R2
    filter to remove individual plants whose growth was not linear.

Panel A statistics
    At each time point, within each site x year group: one-way ANOVA of PSA
    across the five regimes on maternal-line means, followed by Tukey's HSD.
    Only the four contrasts against 100% SWHC are plotted. The value shown is
    -log10 of Tukey's adjusted P, so the four comparisons made at that time point
    are already accounted for.

Panel B statistics
    Within each site x year group and interval: one-way ANOVA of GR across the
    five regimes on maternal-line means, then Tukey's HSD, with groupings
    rendered as compact letters (multcompView::multcompLetters). Letters are
    assigned in order of appearance, not by magnitude, so the letters themselves
    carry no ranking — only which regimes share one.

Panel C statistics
    lm(GR ~ SWHC) fitted separately within each site x year group and interval,
    across all maternal-line means (5 regimes x number of lines). R2 and P are
    those of the regression; P is the P value of the slope.

Intervals
    Early = 144-408 h (23 time points, experiment 20243).
    Late  = 411-747 h (29 time points, experiment 20244).
    These are two separate plant cohorts, not the same plants followed on: the
    plant identifiers differ (Tray01 vs Tray01A) and share no individuals.
    Lines are therefore broken at the 409.5 h boundary rather than joined.

CAVEATS WORTH KEEPING IN THE TEXT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Statistical power. With three maternal lines per regime (two for Oregon 2014),
   Tukey's HSD needs a large difference to reach P < 0.05. The sparseness of the
   -log10(P) rows in panel A is a consequence of line-level replication, not
   evidence of absence.

2. The input tables are spline outputs on a 12 h grid and carry no
   Data.Support column, so the fits include smoothed as well as measured values.
   Smoothing shrinks the residual and the P values are correspondingly
   optimistic. Adequate for ranking traits; not for quoting.

3. The absence of a dose-response in the late interval (panel C, all four groups
   P > 0.1) may not be biological. The spread between 100% and 20% SWHC, as a
   percentage of mean PSA, falls from 44 / 36 / 29 / 5% at the end of the early
   interval (Oregon 2010, California 2014, Oregon 2014, California 2010) to
   27 / 19 / 12 / 19% at the end of the late interval. Three of four groups
   compress. That is what would be expected if neighbouring rosettes began to
   overlap once the plants outgrew the trays, since projected shoot area then
   under-counts the largest plants most. Checking whether the 100% SWHC rosettes
   are touching their neighbours in the late images would separate the two
   explanations.
